# Bite Wise Design Tokens

## Grid and spacing
- Base unit: 4px; primary rhythm: 8px.
- Desktop: 12 columns, 32px outer margin, 24px gutters.
- Tablet: 8 columns, 24px outer margin, 20px gutters.
- Mobile: 4 columns, 16px outer margin, 16px gutters.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px.

## Radius and elevation
- Small 10px, medium 14px, large 20px, extra-large 28px, pill 999px.
- Level 0: border only. Level 1: subtle card shadow. Level 2: sticky navigation. Level 3: dialogs and critical overlays.

## Iconography
- Rounded outlined icons are the default, 2px stroke, 20–24px canvas.
- Filled icons are reserved for selected, critical, or compact status states.
- Icons always include text or an accessible name when meaning is not decorative.

## Illustration
- Modern organic geometry, calm green-led palette, diverse adults and culturally relevant meals.
- Avoid stereotypes, body-shaming imagery, medical alarmism, and overly childish characters.

## Motion
- Page transitions 180–240ms; modal transitions 200ms; progress changes 240–300ms; success feedback 180ms.
- Use standard ease-out for entry and ease-in for exit.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and never require animation to understand state.
